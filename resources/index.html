Books and resources

Stories, song, and movement help the Hoea tō Waka model stay accessible long after a workshop ends.

The bookshelf

Stories that travel with you

My Brother Hemi

Short story · Readers aged 8+ Kaia learns the concepts of Hoea tō Waka from Nan, then uses them while supporting her brother Hemi through bullying at school. The story helps make ideas about thoughts, feelings, identity, compassion, and choice easier to share with young people. Written by Anna Brown and illustrated by Laura King. A useful resource for whānau, teachers, classes, and workshop participants.

Ask about this bookView on Amazon

Maia Makes Waves

Resilience in the real world To friends and family, Maia seems successful and settled. Behind that picture, she is navigating coercion, danger, and systems that do not always understand. Anna shows how the Hoea tō Waka wellbeing model can help Maia find strength and direction while seeking support for her family. The book is intended to increase compassion and understanding for people searching for a way through difficult circumstances. Five dollars from each copy supports NZ Women’s Refuge.

Strength in Te Reo

Five tiles. Six guesses.

✓ Right place↔ Elsewhere– Not in word

Explore this word in the dictionary ↗

How to play

Guess the five-letter word in six tries. Each written letter gets a tile: ng and wh each use two tiles. A vowel with a macron uses one tile and differs from its short vowel: a ≠ ā.

Repeated letters are marked only as many times as they occur in the answer. Guesses must be in this game’s word list; it does not include every Māori word.

Your progress stays in this browser when storage is available. The word order is shuffled, and a word won’t repeat until you finish the set.
