Read the complete prompt

Generate FOUR separate images. Each image must contain a clean 2×2 grid of four distinct variations: 16 variations in total. Do not combine the four output images into one larger grid. Variation plan (top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right in each image): Image 1 — photographic interpretations: candid documentary photography; cinematic environmental photography; tactile analogue-film photography; minimal fine-art studio photography. Image 2 — illustrated interpretations: textured editorial gouache; fluid watercolour and pencil; bold flat vector illustration; expressive ink and pastel. Image 3 — dimensional interpretations: layered cut-paper collage; handmade clay miniature; sculptural translucent glass; soft textile and felt assemblage. Image 4 — experimental interpretations: risograph print; restrained surreal photographic collage; geometric modernist poster artwork without lettering; atmospheric abstract painting that still makes the central idea recognisable. Each panel must be a fresh interpretation, not a duplicate, recolour, filter or minor edit of another panel. Vary composition, viewpoint, lighting, arrangement and visual storytelling as well as medium. Keep the core subject and purpose recognisable in all 16 panels. The scene brief below is the conceptual starting point; reinterpret its photography, material, lighting and camera instructions to suit each assigned style. Preserve meaningful details and any requested negative space within each panel. Scene brief and original inspiration: Create an editorial documentary photograph of fictional adults in contemporary New Zealand. Use natural expressions, believable anatomy, ordinary well-made clothing, tactile everyday surroundings and available light. The atmosphere is warm, capable and unforced. Keep faces and hands anatomically convincing; avoid corporate stock-photo staging. A small group of six adults of varied ages sit in a loose semicircle in a bright, modest Christchurch meeting room. One person speaks while the others listen with relaxed attention. A facilitator sits at the same level. Show coffee cups, unbranded notebooks and a window opening onto soft garden greenery. Frame the group on the right, with a calm plaster wall on the left for a website headline. Art direction: this image is for Hoea tō Waka, a New Zealand resilience training and coaching website. Communicate practical hope, warmth, human connection and space to think. Coordinate with the Tidal Bloom website palette: #175d62, #b65d3e, #8b73b2, #82b6a4, #d9d4c8. Use these colours subtly in clothing, props, light and surroundings; preserve believable skin, vegetation, water and sky colours. Do not apply a heavy overall colour filter. Output: four separate high-resolution 16:9 images, each a 2×2 grid containing four equal-sized 16:9 panels, intended for in-house workshops hero. Use only narrow, consistent neutral gutters between panels, with no decorative outer border. Each panel should be usable as an independent image after cropping: no subjects crossing panel boundaries, and keep essential details clear of panel edges. No text, lettering, panel labels, numbers, logos, watermarks or mockup interface. Do not add a website headline. Deliver four grid images and 16 visibly different style variations in total.
