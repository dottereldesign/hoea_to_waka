In-house resilience workshops

A practical, tailored workshop that helps people find direction, protect their wellbeing, and understand each other better during challenging times.

Workshop for your kaimahi

What to expect

Anna Brown developed Hoea tō Waka to bring mental health and resilience research together with wisdom from te ao Māori. Kaimahi (team members) can use the model without prior training in therapy.

Time together
3.5 hours
Group size
6–12 participants
Fee listed in the workshop flyer
$3,300 + GST per session
Developer and facilitator
Anna Brown
Learning together
01

How your team will learn

Anna uses metaphor, visuals, waiata and movement so people can take part with different learning preferences and levels of literacy.

People already working with therapeutic approaches can use the model alongside them.

02

What you can explore

Topics include setting healthy boundaries, anxiety, relationships, motivation, anger, recognising and addressing bias, and parenting.

Talk with Anna ↗

To confirm the fee for your group or discuss individual training by arrangement.

What the workshop supports

Stronger direction in demanding work

For organisations that want a more compassionate, connected workplace and practical tools staff can use beyond the session.

Wellbeing

Notice needs before overwhelm

Build awareness of energy, stress, self-care, and the choices available during pressure.

Meaning

Connect work with values

Help people identify what matters, create meaningful goals, and find greater clarity in their role.

Connection

Understand each other better

Develop a shared language for behaviour, experience, social context, and more constructive support.

Tailored to the room

One model. Your people and context

Every workplace has different pressures. Before delivery, Anna works with you to understand the audience, the challenges they face, and the language that will make the training useful.

  • Self-care in demanding roles
  • Motivation, direction, and meaningful goals
  • Staff connection, compassion, and retention
  • Moving on from judgement and self-criticism
  • Understanding social inequality, Te Tiriti, and colonisation where appropriate
  • Resources that help the ideas remain active after the workshop

A simple pathway

From enquiry to delivery

  1. 01

    Share your context

    Tell Anna who the workshop is for and what your organisation is navigating.

  2. 02

    Shape the focus

    Agree on the most useful outcomes, examples, and areas of emphasis.

  3. 03

    Deliver and apply

    Participants learn the model interactively and leave with practical resources to keep using it.
