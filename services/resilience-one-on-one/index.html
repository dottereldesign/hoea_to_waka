Focused one-to-one support

Individual coaching

A focused session for leaders or team members who need space to step back, understand what is happening, and choose a useful direction.

Ask about coaching
FormatOne-to-one coaching
Typical sessionApproximately 1½–2 hours
Designed forLeaders, managers, and team members

Clarity and influence

Make room for what matters.

Individual coaching helps a person identify where they have influence, what values matter to them, and which next steps can bring greater focus and wellbeing at work and at home.

The model creates a non-judgemental way to look at thoughts, emotions, behaviour, pressure, and expectations. It can also help a team member connect personal values with the purpose of their organisation.

Discuss a session

Possible outcomes

A practical reset.

The focus is shaped around the person and their role rather than a fixed programme.

01

Greater focus

Separate what can be influenced from the currents that cannot be controlled.

02

Meaningful goals

Turn personal and organisational values into realistic next steps.

03

Sustainable wellbeing

Recognise needs, protect energy, and develop a more compassionate response to pressure.

How it works

Personal, clear, and relevant.

  1. Clarify the need

    Anna and the organisation or individual agree on the purpose and boundaries of the session.

  2. Map what is happening

    Use the Hoea tō Waka model to explore the currents, values, needs, and choices in view.

  3. Choose a direction

    Leave with practical priorities and a way to keep applying the model after the conversation.

Give someone room to find direction.
