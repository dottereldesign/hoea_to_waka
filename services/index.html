A flexible approach

Built around the people in front of us.

Hoea tō Waka is designed to make resilience practical without reducing people or their circumstances to a simple formula. Anna adapts the focus, examples, and delivery to the needs of each person, team, or professional group.

If you are unsure which format fits, begin with a conversation. You do not need to have the programme fully worked out before getting in touch.
