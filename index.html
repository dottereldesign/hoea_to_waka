The model

Designed to stay with you.

The waka metaphor makes complex ideas easier to explore, remember, and apply—at work, at home, and while supporting others.

01

Research translated for everyday use

The model draws on established therapeutic and resilience ideas, then places them in plain language and a memorable visual system.

02

Grounded here in Aotearoa

Whakapapa, tīpuna, culture, spirituality, and lived experience can be included without assuming one way of seeing the world.

03

Built for immediate application

People leave with a shared language for thoughts, emotions, behaviour, values, direction, and the currents around them.

Before you enquire

Frequently Asked Questions

01Do I need to know exactly which service I want?

No. Describe the people involved and the situation you want to support. Anna can help you identify a useful starting point.

02Can a workshop be tailored?

Yes. In-house and support professional training can be shaped around your organisation, audience, and desired outcomes.

03How do I get pricing?

Contact Anna with the likely group, format, and location. She can then provide the most relevant options and a clear quote.

04Can I order books directly?

Yes. Select “Resources or book order” in the contact form, or email Anna directly with the title and quantity you need.

05Who are in-house workshops for?

In-house workshops are tailored for teams and organisations looking for practical resilience tools and a shared language for wellbeing at work.

06What does individual coaching focus on?

Coaching creates space to explore pressure, personal values, and where a person has influence, then identify useful next steps for work and home.

07Is there training for support professionals?

Yes. Training helps people supporting clients, whānau, and communities use the model flexibly, while also caring for their own wellbeing.

08How does the waka metaphor help?

The waka provides a memorable visual language for thoughts, emotions, behaviour, values, and the currents around us, making the model easier to remember and use.
