### Model framing

Current site says Hoea to Waka:

- Honours existing research and translates it for community use
- Is evidence-based
- Is designed especially for Aotearoa
- Can include whakapapa, tipuna, and spiritual beliefs
- Fits both kaupapa Maori and mainstream environments
- Can be applied across self-care, burnout, confidence, difficult thoughts and emotions, goal setting, depression, parenting, motivation, anger, personal growth, supervision, and anxiety
