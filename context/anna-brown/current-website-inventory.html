Sitewide Themes

  • Evidence-based resilience model
  • Designed especially for Aotearoa
  • Culturally grounded
  • Suitable for organisations and individuals
  • Supports wellbeing, self-awareness, and reflective practice

Home Page

Main headline

Hoea to Waka: Strength in Aotearoa

Supporting message

  • Resilience training and resources for organisations and individuals
  • Resilience training set in Aotearoa's unique cultural context

Main service blocks

1. In-house resilience training

  • Half-day workshop
  • Tailored to organisation needs
  • Suitable across industries
  • Staff wellbeing, direction, goal setting, understanding others
  • Can help create more compassionate workplaces

2. Individual coaching

  • For team members
  • Useful for HR, managers, team leaders, or support professionals
  • One-and-a-half to two-hour format
  • Helps leaders support staff, build rapport, and maintain focus/wellbeing

3. Support professional workshops

  • Half-day workshop
  • For social workers, counsellors, health professionals, teachers, youth workers
  • Helps staff support clients while also caring for their own wellbeing
  • Includes exploration of social inequalities, colonisation, and Te Tiriti

Resources section

  • My Brother Hemi as a take-home learning resource
  • Waiata-a-ringa and English-language song
  • Multisensory reinforcement of workshop concepts

About Page

Founder bio

  • Spent eight years as a youth worker
  • Worked with young people convicted of sexual offences
  • Worked with Youth Guarantee students
  • Spent ten years counselling people aged thirteen and up
  • Worked in private practice and secondary schools
  • Is the mother of five children
  • Has Scottish, Irish, English and Ngai Tahu / Ngai Tuahuriri descent

Model framing

  • Honours existing research and translates it for community use
  • Is evidence-based
  • Is designed especially for Aotearoa
  • Can include whakapapa, tipuna, and spiritual beliefs
  • Fits both kaupapa Maori and mainstream environments
  • Can be applied across self-care, burnout, confidence, difficult thoughts and emotions, goal setting, depression, parenting, motivation, anger, personal growth, supervision, and anxiety

In-house Workshops Page

  • For managers, supervisors, team leaders, and HR professionals
  • Self-care during challenging times
  • Meaning, clarity, and satisfaction in work
  • Values-aligned work and motivation
  • Support for staff retention
  • Burnout prevention
  • Social inequalities, Te Tiriti, and colonisation as topics of exploration
  • Option to design workshops or coaching around specific support needs

Individual Coaching Page

  • One-on-one coaching for team members
  • Focus, resilience, and wellbeing
  • Prioritisation and influence
  • Alignment between personal values and organisational values
  • Motivation, meaning, and staff retention
  • Goal setting important to both the person and the organisation

Support Professionals Page

  • For social workers, medical professionals, counsellors, youth workers, psychologists, and teachers
  • Helps clients find power and meaningful goals
  • Builds rapport in flexible and culturally relevant ways
  • Non-confrontational exploration of thoughts, emotions, and behaviour
  • Compatible with existing models
  • References third-wave therapies: Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, Compassion Focused Therapy, Metacognitive Therapy
  • Helps deepen reflective practice
  • Also builds staff resilience
