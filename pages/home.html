Detailed Section Map

1. Header and navigation

  • Block type: Site Parts
  • Includes brand/title link, tagline, primary navigation, and services dropdown
  • Site title should be linked brand text or a logo, not a numbered page heading
  • Interactive items are standard nav links, not buttons

2. Hero

  • Block type: Hero
  • Supporting blocks: Content, Image Box
  • h1: Hoea tō Waka: Strength in Aotearoa
  • Supporting line stays the same, but should be paragraph text rather than a heading
  • Full-width hero image
  • No button currently

3. Services intro

  • Block type: Heading
  • Supporting block: Content
  • h2: Resilience training set in Aotearoa's unique cultural context.

4. Services row

  • Best-fit block type: Features
  • Why: it is a three-column section made of repeatable offer cards with copy plus CTA buttons

Service card 1

  • h3: In-house resilience training for your organisation
  • Button: Go to inhouse workshops

Service card 2

  • h3: Individual coaching for your team members
  • Button: Go to individual coaching

Service card 3

  • h3: The Hoea tō Waka model for support professionals
  • Button: More for support professionals

5. Testimonial slider

  • Block type: Testimonial
  • Supporting block: Sliders
  • Rotating quote cards, previous arrow, next arrow, and slider dots
  • If the visible content remains quotes only, do not force an unnecessary heading level into the section

6. Resources and book section

  • Best-fit block type: Full Width Duo
  • Supporting blocks: Content, Image Box, Call To Action
  • Left side includes h2 Resources, supporting copy, and See resources button
  • Right side includes book cover image, h3 NEW BOOK out now, and the NZ Women's Refuge donation message as supporting text

7. Contact section

  • Block type: Contact
  • Includes contact details and contact form
  • h2: Contact
  • Static text includes Hoea tō Waka: Strength in Aotearoa and Based in Ōtautahi Christchurch.
  • Fields: name, email, phone, message
  • Button: Send

8. Social row

  • Block type: Social
  • Items: Facebook, Twitter/X, Instagram, YouTube
  • Appears in the footer/contact area

9. Footer strip

  • Block type: Site Parts
  • Contains copyright text
  • Example: ©2023 Hoea to Waka Training Ltd.
