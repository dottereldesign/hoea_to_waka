What we learned from this reconstruction

The people and objects are individually editable vector shapes. The outlines were drawn in the reference’s coordinate system, checked with a 50% overlay, and refined against visible colour boundaries. Colours were sampled away from edges, then fitted to gentle gradients.

Overlay checks caught differences in the sun rings, mat and clothing curves. Aggressive automatic edge snapping also introduced small bumps at overlaps; those changes were rejected in favour of smoother authored curves. The result is a close reconstruction, with small contour and shading differences still visible at high magnification.

The experimental grain is independent of the drawing. The first four texture choices use the existing texture images; Pure SVG grain uses a procedural filter. Recolouring a material replaces its original gradient with your chosen colour. The hover test lifts complete groups, so small amounts of overlap can become visible.

For another illustration, start with named layers and shared boundaries, compare at the original size before adding grain, and save clean, overlay and difference images after every substantial change.
