Topics for Anna and Jamie to review together and decide what to do next.

Anna

  • Brand overview
  • Review wording. Flag anything on the site that you would phrase differently.
  • Memberships page. Send details on how membership works, what members get, and how payments through Stripe will work.
  • Testimonials. Please send through any more testimonials you would like to share on the site.
  • Homepage FAQ. Please review the questions and answers at https://dottereldesign.github.io/hoea_to_waka/#home-faq
  • About page. I would love to hear about your journey, both in and outside of work. Where would you start your story? Which people, places, roles or turning points have shaped you along the way? Approximate years for key moments would be helpful if you remember them.
  • Model page. Review the text and decide what to move, rewrite, or remove.

Jamie

  • Keynote talks (for later). Research what keynote talks are for and why event organisers book them. Find out what organisers around Christchurch and across New Zealand typically pay for a keynote, including the average fee if figures are available.
  • Anna's review list. Make a to-do list with screenshots showing the specific wording Anna should review.
  • International SEO. Look into how people overseas can find the site.
  • Content for overseas visitors. Explore using a visitor's approximate country to offer extra context outside Aotearoa New Zealand, such as short explanations of Māori terms and cultural references. Let visitors choose either version themselves.
  • Sponsors. Create a placeholder for a sponsor carousel on the homepage for Anna to review. Let Anna know she could also acknowledge sponsors in her articles and newsletters. These are ways to recognise support and open conversations about funding and partnerships.
