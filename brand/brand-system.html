Brand System

Working brand system for the Hoea tō Waka rebuild.

Brand Summary

  • Brand name: Hoea tō Waka
  • Core line in current use: Strength in Aotearoa
  • Overall feel: calm, supportive, practical, grounded in Aotearoa, people-first rather than corporate
  • Current site mood: light, oceanic, airy, service-led

Buttons

Use rounded 12px corners and a minimum 48px height. Primary actions use Ocean 800, Ocean 700 on hover and Ocean 900 when pressed. Disabled actions use Ocean 300. Secondary actions use Forest outlines on light backgrounds. On dark surfaces, use Lavender with Aubergine text.

Use right arrows for internal navigation when helpful, and visible Ocean 600 keyboard focus rings. Colour transitions take 150–200 ms.

Layout Patterns

  • Full-width image hero
  • Centered section intro
  • Three-column service grid
  • Full-width testimonial band in blue
  • Split content-and-image promo block
  • Contact details paired with form

Imagery Direction

  • Water
  • Movement
  • Journey
  • Resilience
  • Aotearoa context
  • Waka / waterway associations
  • Coastal or open-sky tones
  • Human, practical, non-corporate imagery
  • Resource-led imagery such as books and workshop material
  • Kayak / waka associations and wahine-led continuity

Voice and Tone

  • Warm
  • Respectful
  • Clear
  • Grounded
  • Supportive
  • Locally rooted

It should avoid sounding overly clinical, generic corporate wellness, or overly spiritual without practical grounding.
