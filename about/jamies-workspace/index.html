Final website review

Items for confirmation

Please review each item and send any corrections or approvals in one reply. Short answers are completely fine.

  1. Professional credentials

    Confirm the qualifications, professional memberships, role titles, and any other credentials that may be published.

  2. Workshop logistics

    Confirm the standard workshop duration, typical group size, delivery format, and any venue or equipment requirements.

  3. Delivery locations

    Confirm the regions covered for in-person delivery and whether online or nationwide delivery is available.

  4. Pricing approach

    Confirm whether pricing should be published, presented as “from” pricing, or supplied on enquiry.

  5. Shielded Site and Women’s Refuge wording

    Confirm that the Shielded Site badge should remain in the footer. Please also approve the exact wording of any statement about support for, or donations to, Women’s Refuge.

  6. Testimonial approval

    Approve the client testimonials selected for the website and confirm permission to publish any names, roles, or organisations shown.

Helpful response format

Reply with Approved for each item that is correct, or provide the replacement wording or details beneath that heading.
