About Hoea tō Waka

Grounded support. Practical movement.

Hoea tō Waka is a resilience model designed for Aotearoa—bringing research, cultural context, and real-world practice together in a form people can remember and use.

FounderAnna Brown
CredentialsBA (Psych), Dip Counselling, MNZAC
Based inŌtautahi Christchurch

Founder and programme developer

Meet Anna Brown

Anna spent eight years as a youth worker, including work with young people convicted of sexual offences and Youth Guarantee students. She then spent ten years counselling people aged thirteen and over in private practice and secondary schools.

That experience shaped a clear need: translate useful resilience research into something people, teams, and communities could understand without specialist language.

Anna is a mother of five and is of Scottish, Irish, English, and Ngāi Tahu (Ngāi Tūāhuriri) descent. Her work holds professional evidence and cultural context together with warmth, humility, and practical purpose.

The model

Designed to stay with you.

The waka metaphor makes complex ideas easier to explore, remember, and apply—at work, at home, and while supporting others.

01 / Evidence

Research translated for everyday use

The model draws on established therapeutic and resilience ideas, then places them in plain language and a memorable visual system.

02 / Context

Grounded here in Aotearoa

Whakapapa, tīpuna, culture, spirituality, and lived experience can be included without assuming one way of seeing the world.

03 / Practice

Built for immediate application

People leave with a shared language for thoughts, emotions, behaviour, values, direction, and the currents around them.

Where it can help

A flexible model for real life.

Hoea tō Waka can support reflection and action across a wide range of situations.

  • Self-care
  • Burnout prevention
  • Confidence
  • Values
  • Goal setting
  • Motivation
  • Difficult emotions
  • Parenting
  • Professional supervision
  • Understanding others
  • Personal growth
  • Anxiety
