Founder and programme developer

Meet Anna Brown

BA (Psych), Dip Counselling, MNZAC

Experience with young people, a decade of counselling, and a clear aim: make resilience research useful in everyday life.

01 pt 2 · Ocean passageAn ocean passage cut through white paper. Follow the waka down Anna’s story.

  1. 01Youth work · 8 years

    Alongside young people

    Anna spent eight years as a youth worker, including work with young people convicted of sexual offences and Youth Guarantee students.

  2. 02Counselling · 10 years

    Space to talk, room to move

    She then spent ten years counselling people aged thirteen and over, in private practice and secondary schools.

  3. 03A clear need

    Research people can carry

    That experience shaped a clear need: translate useful resilience research into something people, teams, and communities could understand without specialist language.

  4. 04Personal roots

    Grounded in who she is

    Anna is a mother of five and is of Scottish, Irish, English, and Ngāi Tahu (Ngāi Tūāhuriri) descent.

  5. 05The work today

    Warmth, evidence, purpose

    Her work holds professional evidence and cultural context together with warmth, humility, and practical purpose.
